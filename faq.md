# Gomla.eg — Frequently Asked Questions

## 1. What are Gomla.eg opening hours?

Gomla.eg customer support is available every day from 10:00 AM to 10:00 PM Cairo time, except on official public holidays.

## 2. Does Gomla.eg ship to all Egyptian governorates?

Yes. Gomla.eg ships orders to all governorates in Egypt, including Cairo, Giza, Alexandria, Dakahlia, Gharbia, Sharqia, Qalyubia, and Aswan.

## 3. How long does delivery take?

Orders within Cairo and Giza usually arrive within 1–2 business days. Orders to other Egyptian governorates usually arrive within 2–5 business days.

## 4. How much does shipping cost?

Shipping costs 50 EGP for Cairo and Giza and 70 EGP for other Egyptian governorates. Shipping is free for orders above 1,000 EGP.

## 5. What payment methods are available?

Gomla.eg accepts cash on delivery, Visa, Mastercard, and eligible local electronic payment methods available at checkout.

## 6. Can I pay cash when my order arrives?

Yes. Cash on delivery is available for eligible orders in Egypt. Some products or orders may require online payment.

## 7. What is Gomla.eg's return policy?

Customers can request a return within 14 days of receiving an eligible product. The product must be unused and in its original condition and packaging. Some products may be excluded from the return policy.

## 8. What should I do if I receive a damaged or incorrect product?

Contact Gomla.eg customer support as soon as possible and provide the order number and photos of the damaged or incorrect product. The support team will review the case and arrange the appropriate solution.

## 9. Does Gomla.eg provide support in Arabic and English?

Yes. Gomla.eg customer support can assist customers in both Arabic and English.

## 10. How can I track my order?

After an order is shipped, Gomla.eg provides the customer with tracking information when tracking is available for the selected delivery service.
